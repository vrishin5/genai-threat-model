# Acme Support FAQ

**Q: What are your support hours?**
A: Our support team is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm ET.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Go to Settings > Security > Reset Password. You'll receive a confirmation
email within a few minutes.

**Q: Where can I see my order history?**
A: Order history is available under Account > Orders. Orders older than
24 months are archived and can be requested from support.

**Q: How do I contact a human agent?**
A: Email support@acme.example or use the in-app chat and type "agent".
